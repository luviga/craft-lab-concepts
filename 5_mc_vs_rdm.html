Monte Carlo asks how likely stress is. Robust Decision Making (RDM) asks under which conditions stress happens.

Monte Carlo needs a probability for each input. RDM needs only a range. A Monte Carlo probability speaks about the world. An RDM share speaks about the chosen bounds.
